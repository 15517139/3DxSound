## ✨ What is 3DxSound?

3DxSound plays a different ambience or a live DJ set in each area of your 3DXChat room, **automatically**, as soon as a coloured panel appears on your screen. Everyone who joins your room hears the same sounds.

A forest path with birdsong, a beach with waves, a dance floor with a live DJ, a lounge with its own playlist… With 3DxSound, one 3DXChat room can host **several ambiences and several DJs at the same time**, each in its own area. More atmosphere, more reasons to stay, more people.

## 🎨 A coloured panel = a sound

Each room has up to **5 sounds**, one per colour: 🟩 green, 🩷 pink, 🟦 blue, 🟧 orange and 🟥 red. You choose which colour goes with which sound.
Place the panels in your room with the 3DXChat editor. When a panel appears on your screen, 3DxSound starts its sound; walk to another area and the sound switches by itself.
The panel file also includes the **3DxSound title panel**. Place it at the entrance of your room: as soon as they arrive, players know the room has several ambient sounds, which they hear if they start 3DxSound with this room.

## ⏱️ Everyone hears the same moment

The sounds of the 3DxSound pack are **synchronized**: from the moment a room is opened, its ambiences and DJ sets play like a real radio. Arrive at any time, walk from one area to another, come back later: you always land exactly where the track really is, at the same second as everyone else in the room.

## 👀 See where the party is

Each room in the list shows two badges: the landscape gives the number of different ambiences, the little figure the number of **listeners connected right now**. Counts update live, so you instantly see which rooms are busy.

## ⭐ Your favourite rooms, always on top

Three small buttons sort the room list the way you like. Nothing to configure: your choices are kept on your PC.

- ⭐ **Favourites**: click the star in front of a room to make it a favourite. Switch on the big star above the list: your favourites go to the top, in alphabetical order.
- 🔵 **Sort by listeners**: switch on the blue **1** button: the busiest rooms come first. The order is refreshed every 15 seconds, so the list never jumps under your mouse.
- 📎 **Paper clip on your room**: only you see it, next to your own open room. On (by default): your room stays at the very top of the list. Off: it is sorted like all the other rooms.

Order of the list: your room (paper clip on), the ticked room, your favourites (star on), then all the others.

## 🎚️ Change your sounds live

A new DJ takes over, a radio goes offline, you want another ambience? As the creator, you don't need to close your room. Click the green **Mix** button next to the minimized 3DxSound tab, choose the colour of the sound, paste a new stream link or pick another sound from the pack — you can even turn a stream into a pack sound, or the other way round — and save: everyone in the room switches to it at once, in sync.

## 📖 How it works

### 1. Install

1. **[Download the installer](https://github.com/15517139/3DxSound/releases/latest/download/3DxSound-setup.exe)** and run it.
2. Choose your language: 3DxSound will start in that language (you can change it at any time in the app).
3. The default installation folder is `C:\3DxSound`. The installer comes with an empty sound pack: on first launch, 3DxSound tells you how to download the full pack (see step 8).

### 2. Set up the game

3DxSound watches the game screen, so 3DXChat must be displayed correctly:

- **Graphics**: set **Display mode** to **Fullscreen window**.
- **Audio**: turn **Music** down to the minimum so the game music does not cover 3DxSound.

### 3. Place the panels in your room

- In the installation folder, the **`Read-Panel-Room-Build`** folder contains **`ColorRead-3DxSound`**: the file with the 5 panels (`.world`) and a photo of them.
- In the 3DXChat room editor, open the menu and choose **Merge**, then select this file: the panels are added to your room without removing anything.
- Put each panel where its sound should play: at the entrance of an area, on a wall, near the DJ booth…
- Keep the **black square** around the triangle: 3DxSound only reacts to the complete panel, so lamps and lights of the same colour never trigger a sound.
- The sound starts as soon as the panel is visible on your screen, and changes when another panel appears.

> [!NOTE]
> **Demo room:** the `Read-Panel-Room-Build` folder also contains **`ROOM 3DxSound Demo`**, a demonstration room (`.world` file) with 4 photos. Load it in the 3DXChat room editor to see an example of a room made for 3DxSound.

> [!NOTE]
> **Title panel:** the `ColorRead-3DxSound` file also contains the “Compatible 3DxSound” title panel. Put it at the entrance of your room: when they come in, players know this room has several ambient sounds, which they will hear if they start 3DxSound with this room.

### 5. Create your room

1. **MY ROOMS** tab: click **ADD**. Your rooms are kept on your PC. **OPEN** makes your room visible to everyone, **CLOSE** removes it. Quitting 3DxSound closes your room. One open room per PC.
2. Name your room. Switch **Program** on to write a text (250 characters max) shown to everyone under the sounds. Add up to 5 sounds and pick the arrival sound. The room window opens attached to the interface, and the sound window attached to the room window.
3. **Triangle colour**: open the menu at the top of the sound window. Green by default; colours already used by another sound of the room are greyed out.
4. **STREAM**: a direct link to an online audio stream (radio, .mp3 or .ogg file), like the personal links of the 3DXChat radio.
5. **LOCAL**: a sound from the sound pack installed on the PC. The public name is optional; if it is empty, the file name is shown.
6. **Choose a sound**: Ambience, Playlist and DJ, one search bar for all three columns. ► to listen before choosing.
7. **Add request**: fill in the form (sounds of at least 60 minutes only), click **Copy** then **Post**. The Discord opens: right-click › Paste, and send.

#### Change a sound without closing the room

While your room is open, you can change any of its sounds at any time: the name and link of a **STREAM** sound, or the pack sound and public name of a **LOCAL** sound. Only you, the creator, see the **Stream**, **Local** and **Mix** buttons:

- **Minimized**: a green **Mix** button sits next to the 3DxSound tab (the background around the buttons is transparent). It opens a window in the middle of the screen whose menu lists **all** the colours of the room, each marked (STREAM) or (LOCAL); the window adapts to the sound you pick. Click **Mix** again to close it.
- **Interface open**: in the **ROOM** tab, click your room: a green **Stream** button appears next to each STREAM sound and a cyan **Local** button next to each LOCAL sound.
- **Stream** → *Change a stream*: the STREAM sounds, change the name and the streaming link.
- **Local** → *Change a local sound*: the LOCAL sounds, **Choose a sound** opens the sound pack (with preview); the public name is optional.
- **STREAM / LOCAL boxes** (in all three windows): change the type of the sound — replace a stream with a pack sound (an ambience, a DJ set…) or the other way round.
- **Save**: the room stays open, the change is kept in MY ROOMS, and everyone listening switches to the new sound right away (LOCAL sounds stay synchronized). **Cancel**: nothing changes.

### 7. Forced app / web / folder

A red gear marks a program, a purple planet a website, an orange folder a folder.

1. Click a name to open it, click again to close it. Each block has its switch (button in the minimized tab), the pencil and the red **X**. Under a program, the green **Start** button becomes a red **Stop** once it runs. Hold a block and drag it to change its place.
2. **Add app**: the application name (also shown on its minimized button), then **Browse…** to pick the program (.exe, shortcut, script…).
3. **Add web**: the tab name (also shown on its minimized button) and the URL link.
4. **Add folder**: the folder name, then **Browse…** to pick it with Windows File Explorer.

### 8. Updates

#### Sound pack

1. A new sound pack is available: a red message tells you when 3DxSound starts. Click **OK**: **+ Options** and **Pack update** open by themselves.
2. The list of packs (in **+ Options** › **Pack update**). ✓ installed, ⬇ the next one to download, 🔒 locked until the previous one is installed. Packs install in order.
3. Click the pack name: a window shows its size. Click **Download**.
4. The download runs inside the app with a progress bar, then the pack installs by itself.
5. Another pack is waiting? 3DxSound offers to download it right away. **YES** continues, **NO** stops here.
6. Everything is installed: restart. **Automatic restart** relaunches 3DxSound for you.

#### Application

When 3DxSound starts, it checks for a new version. **The update is required: an older version can no longer be used.** Your sound pack and your rooms are kept: the update installs in the same folder, in the same language, without any question, then 3DxSound restarts by itself. If a new version and a new sound pack are available together, the app is updated first; the pack is offered at the next start.

1. A new version is available: the red message tells you the update is required. **YES** starts it, **NO** quits 3DxSound.
2. The new version downloads with a progress bar. **Cancel** deletes the unfinished file and quits 3DxSound. Then the installer runs on its own and 3DxSound restarts, up to date.
